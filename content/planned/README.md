# Planned animals

Animals on the globe that don't have a pack yet. They show as greyed,
untappable "coming soon" pins. When an animal's pack lands in
`content/animals/<id>/`, delete its entry here (the validator refuses an
animal that is in both places).

Icons are stand-ins from Fluent Emoji Flat (MIT, see `icons/LICENSE-fluentui-emoji`).
Animals without an emoji of their own borrow a near one until real art
exists: Philippine eagle and bald eagle (eagle), tamaraw (water buffalo),
whale shark (shark), toco toucan and Atlantic puffin (bird), platypus
(otter), narwhal (whale), chameleon (lizard), jaguar (leopard).
